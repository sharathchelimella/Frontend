Creation & Development

  • Initial Conception: Lee wanted to create a new type of hero. Inspired by a fly crawling on a wall, he conceived "Spider-Man" as a character who could stick to surfaces.
  • The "Teen" Problem: Publisher Martin Goodman initially rejected the idea, arguing teenagers could only be sidekicks and that people hated spiders.
  • First Appearance: Lee, convinced of the idea, put the character in the final issue of Amazing Fantasy (#15) in 1962. It was an instant hit, leading to his own series.
  • Visual Design: Artist Steve Ditko is responsible for the iconic red-and-blue costume, web-shooters, and the acrobatic, slender look of the character
